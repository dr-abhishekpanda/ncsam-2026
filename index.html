      My Cyber Hygiene Pledge

      Make it personal. Add your name and download a pledge card to share.

      • I will pause, check and verify before I click, download or respond.
      • I will never share my passwords, PINs or OTPs with anyone.
      • I will use strong, unique passwords and turn on MFA.
      • I will install updates promptly and use only genuine software.
      • I will report suspicious messages and cyber incidents, not forward them.

      Report Cyber Security Incidents

      Noticed unusual activity, a suspicious message or a compromised account?
      Report it promptly to CERT-In.

      Helpline1930

      Lost money, or being threatened on a call?

      Call 1930, the national cybercrime helpline, straight away. It works day and night, and the sooner you report, the better the chance of stopping the money before it reaches the fraudsters. You can also report any cybercrime online at cybercrime.gov.in, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

      India's citizen fraud-reporting system had saved more than ₹7,130 crore by 31 October 2025 (Ministry of Home Affairs).
