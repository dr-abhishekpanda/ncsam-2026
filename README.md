# Build a Culture of Cyber Hygiene - NCSAM 2026

An interactive, offline-ready public awareness page for **National Cyber Security Awareness Month (October 2026)**. #CyberHygieneOurCulture

**Live page:** https://dr-abhishekpanda.github.io/ncsam-2026/

## What's inside
- **30 Days of Cyber Hygiene**: the daily Cybersecurity Best Practices and Cyber Smart Tips
- **A to Z Do's & Don'ts** flip cards and a "Do or Don't?" speed round
- **AI-Powered Threats**: phishing, deepfakes, AI-assisted malware, fake websites, plus a Red Flags checklist
- **Play Zone**: 9 scenario-based challenges from the CyberSmart Classroom guide
- **Annual Security Checklist** and a downloadable **Cyber Hygiene Pledge** card
- **Report**: the national cybercrime helpline **1930** and cybercrime.gov.in for anyone who has lost money or is being threatened, plus CERT-In's incident-reporting contacts

The whole site is a single `index.html` with fonts and images embedded, so it works offline: download the file and open it in any browser, or share it on WhatsApp or by USB. Progress and checklist ticks stay in the visitor's own browser, and nothing is sent anywhere. The only other file, `preview.png`, is the picture social media shows when the link is shared.

## Source
Content adapted from CERT-In's NCSAM 2026 awareness resources: *Cyber Security Do's and Don'ts: An A to Z Guide*, the *AI Awareness Guide*, *CyberSmart Classroom: Scenario Based Cybersecurity Guide for Teachers & Students*, and the 30-day poster series. The example emails, calls, chat prompts and web addresses in the games are fictional practice material.

This is not an official website of CERT-In, MeitY or the Government of India. Report cyber security incidents to CERT-In: incident@cert-in.org.in · Toll-free +91-1800-11-4949 · https://www.cert-in.org.in

Lost money to a cyber fraud, or being threatened on a call? Call the national cybercrime helpline **1930** straight away, or report at https://cybercrime.gov.in.
